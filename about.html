THE STORY

Design With a Business Mindset.

I’m Dominick Birge, founder of Dominate Websites. I started building business websites because too many local companies either had outdated sites, sites that did not represent the quality of their work, or no website at all.

My approach is simple: create something that looks professional, loads quickly, works perfectly on mobile, and gives customers a clear reason to call, message, or request a quote.

I handle the process from concept and mockup through development, launch, and ongoing updates so business owners can stay focused on running their business.
